## How to review a case

1. Open `form.pdf` next to `sources/intake_notes.txt`.
2. For every entry in `expected.json`, check that:
   - a non-null value is what a careful human would enter from the intake notes;
   - a `null` is right (the notes don't state it, or it is office-use / signature / not applicable).
3. Fix `expected.json` (and the notes, if a sentence is ambiguous). Keep `persona.json` as the raw
   generator output for provenance.
4. Mark the case reviewed below.
